Every Story Ends With What It Means for You

Each report in this paper runs in three parts: what happened, why it matters, and one honest line on whether Rudy can use it, build on it or earn from it. When a story offers nothing to act on, the paper says so plainly instead of inventing an angle. Claims that come from a single outlet are marked Reported; anything confirmed by an official source or two independent outlets is marked Verified.
